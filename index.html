THE BUG STRIKES BACK

The Galactic Code Wars rage on. In the far reaches of the digital cosmos, three formidable batches of students emerge from the depths of their coding bootcamps. Web Dev Padawans, wielding the power of Ruby on Rails, JavaScript, SQL, and HTML, prepare to join forces with the Data Knights—masters of Python, Keras, TensorFlow, and Pandas.

Together, they form the Bootcamp Alliance, bound by a shared mission: to protect the galaxy from the ever-growing forces of the Bug Empire. Led by the legendary Ruby General and the Python Sage, these coders have battled endless lines of code, debugging loops, and cryptic error messages with the precision of a well-placed semicolon.

But the Bug Empire is relentless, spreading glitches and runtime errors across the galaxy with every deploy. Its latest weapon? The feared 'Infinite Loop', a terror so powerful it threatens to trap even the most seasoned coder in an eternal state of confusion.

Armed with their frameworks and libraries, the Web Dev Padawans and Data Knights join forces. Their combined powers of elegant backends and predictive algorithms create digital defenses no empire can breach. As the countdown to the ultimate showdown approaches, they must now prepare for the Final Merge, a deployment so monumental, it will decide the fate of the galaxy.

Will the Bootcamp Alliance's code pass the final review, or will the Bug Empire's errors cause their systems to crash? Only time—and their test coverage—will tell.

The future of the galaxy rests in their hands... And as they march toward the final battle, keyboards in hand, they know one thing for sure: The Force is Strong with These Batches.
